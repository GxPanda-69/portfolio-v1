random outcome

random outcome is an upcoming Roblox game primarly made by me.

It is a free-for-all combat game, which focuses around the unpredictability of what will happen each round. Let me explain:

  • Each round happens on a randomly selected map
  • The items are also given randomly, with one of each class, melee, distance and miscellaneous
  • Two modifiers are also selected (e.g. low gravity, high walk speed, only melee...)
  • One can buy additional modifiers to the game (but not select them, only add to the stack)

With all of that combined plus a moderate competitive aspect, with a global leaderboard reset every two months or so, I hope that the game will create a dedicated community, like PHIGHTING! for example.
